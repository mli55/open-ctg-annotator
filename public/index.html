Typing criteria (NICHD/FIGO): early mirrors the contraction, gradual, nadir aligned with the peak; late gradual but onset/nadir/recovery shifted after the contraction (nadir past the peak); variable abrupt drop and recovery (<30 s to nadir), variable shape, inconsistent relation to contractions; prolonged below baseline ≥2 min and <10 min. Leave an event unclassified (dashed) when you cannot decide or classification is not applicable (e.g. the contraction channel is unreadable, so early vs late cannot be judged). Detector prefill provides intervals only: all classification is yours. Annotations are one shared copy: enter your name (top right) before editing — every action is attributed and listed in the record’s History panel.
